# Playbook — Cross-Department Sensitive Access (CROSS_DEPT_SENSITIVE)

## What this Finding means

The person accessed confidential or restricted files owned by another department, above their normal level. People rarely need another department's sensitive records, so this may mean overly broad permissions or a need-to-know question.

## Common legitimate explanations

- Cross-functional project work, such as Finance supporting an HR payroll change.
- A new role or temporary assignment not yet reflected in permissions.
- Audit, compliance or legal review duties.
- Files mislabelled with the wrong owning department or sensitivity.

## Verification steps

1. Identify which departments and file categories were accessed.
2. Check for an active cross-functional project or assignment.
3. Check whether the access was viewing only, or included downloads or transfers.
4. Check the file's sensitivity label and owning department for labelling errors.

## Recommended actions

- Document the Finding and the triage outcome.
- Check the access against project assignments and role changes.
- **[approval required]** Ask the owning department's data steward, through the Reviewer, to confirm need-to-know.
- **[approval required]** Review and, if appropriate, reduce the person's access to that department's sensitive repositories.

## Escalation

Viewing only, with a plausible project link, is usually Medium. Downloads or transfers of restricted files from several departments call for the High-risk steps.
