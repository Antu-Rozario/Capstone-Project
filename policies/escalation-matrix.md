# Escalation Matrix

How the Risk Score level decides the response. Timelines are in business days. Every action marked **[approval required]** has no effect until a Reviewer approves the case.

## Low risk

Activity is within or close to the person's normal pattern, or only supporting evidence exists.

- Record the detection. No case summary is needed.
- Continue routine monitoring.
- No contact with the person or their manager.

## Medium risk

At least one Finding with a clear deviation from baseline.

- The Analyst completes triage within **2 business days**.
- A case summary is prepared and submitted for review.
- Allowed without approval: documenting the case, checking it against known business events (deadlines, projects, on-call rotas), and continued monitoring.
- **[approval required]** Asking the line manager, through the Reviewer, to confirm the business justification.
- Close the case if a legitimate explanation is confirmed.

## High risk

Several Findings, or one severe deviation such as a very large transfer volume or transfers to an unmanaged device.

- The Analyst completes triage within **1 business day**. The Reviewer decides within **1 further business day**.
- Allowed without approval: preserving relevant logs under the Evidence Handling Standard.
- **[approval required]** Confirming the business justification with the line manager.
- **[approval required]** Temporarily restricting removable-media or external-sharing permissions while the review is ongoing.
- **[approval required]** Referring to HR, and to Legal where regulated or contractual data may be involved.
- **[approval required]** Re-identifying the pseudonymous subject, when needed to carry out an approved action.

## Closing a case

Every case closes with one of: *legitimate activity confirmed*, *data quality issue*, *policy reminder issued* or *escalated*. Rejected and closed cases are kept to measure false-positive rates by department and role (Privacy and Fairness Standard, Fairness monitoring).
