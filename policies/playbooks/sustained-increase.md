# Playbook — Sustained Increase in Transfers (SUSTAINED_INCREASE)

## What this Finding means

Over a rolling 14-day window, the person's total file transfers were well above their baseline, even though no single day was a spike. A gradual, sustained increase can go unnoticed by daily thresholds.

## Common legitimate explanations

- A longer project phase, such as a product launch or audit season.
- A changed role that involves more document handling.
- Gradually adopting offline or local workflows.
- Covering for an absent colleague.

## Verification steps

1. Compare the 14-day total with the baseline expectation in the Finding.
2. Check whether the increase covers own-department working files or extends to sensitive or other-department files.
3. Look for project or role changes during the window.
4. Check whether the trend continues or has stopped.

## Recommended actions

- Document the Finding and the triage outcome.
- Continue monitoring the trend for a further 14 days.
- **[approval required]** Confirm the business justification with the line manager through the Reviewer.

## Escalation

A sustained increase alone is usually Medium. It becomes High when combined with removable media, an unmanaged device, after-hours timing or a planned departure.
